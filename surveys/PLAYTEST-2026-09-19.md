# Playtest — 2026-09-19

Two fresh worlds on `main` at `5f46add`, NeoForge 26.2, dedicated server + connected
client (the client is the eyes; commands went in over RCON so a step burst could not be
mistyped). Both worlds generated from nothing, no staging, cheats via `op Dev`.

| Run | Seed | World | Town watched |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 8675309 | `neoforge/run/ptA` | **Wilbury**, human/vale, `ring_streets`, raised at world start, grown to step ~1500 (pop 91) |
| B | 20260919 | `neoforge/run/ptB` | **Thornring**, human/vale, `ring_streets`, grown to step ~390 (pop 36) |

**Harness fault found before the first run.** `neoforge/run/config/civilization-server.toml`
was left at `worldgen.region = 512` by an earlier session, not the shipped default of 1024.
The first world generated under it and measured the wrong density (nearest town 304 blocks,
minimum separation 320). I set it back to 1024 and regenerated. Anybody playtesting this
checkout should check that file first — a run under it silently tests the wrong dial.

Other harness notes: `spawn-monsters=false`, `generate-structures=false`, `difficulty=normal`,
`view-distance=12`. The client's `options.txt` has `mainHand:"left"`, which is why the held
wayfinder sits on the left of every screenshot; that is not a mod fault.

## 1. Spacing — FAIL

**Run A join message, verbatim** (`neoforge/run/logs/latest.log`, newlines unescaped):

```
Settlements within 2048 blocks:
  Wilbury — 744 blocks NW
  a Burgher radial concentric — 783 blocks SW (not raised yet)
  a Norman green — 1456 blocks SW (not raised yet)
  a Highland thorp — 1493 blocks SE (not raised yet)
  a Vale ring streets — 1547 blocks NW (not raised yet)
```

**Run B join message, verbatim:**

```
Settlements within 2048 blocks:
  Thornring — 802 blocks NW
  a goblin camp — 1693 blocks NW (not raised yet) — hostile
  a Burgher crossroads — 1916 blocks SW (not raised yet)
```

Exactly one town is raised on join in both worlds, and in both it is the only settlement
inside 1024 — that half of the promise holds. The distance does not.

`/civ sites` and the `WORLDGEN` lines say why. In **both** worlds the spawn region's own site
landed inside the 256–512 band and was then **refused for ground**, and the fallback took over:

| Run | Spawn | Home region site | Its distance | Outcome | Town actually raised | Its distance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | (16, 64, 80) | r(0,0) x=320 z=320, `human/burgher radial_concentric` | **387 m** (in band) | `refused: no ground for a town near (320, 0, 320)` | Wilbury (−503, 108, −457) | **747 m** |
| B | (−32, 75, 128) | r(−1,0) x=−320 z=320, `orc/warhost orc_ring` | **346 m** (in band) | `refused: no ground for a town near (−320, 0, 320)` | Thornring (−468, 81, −552) | **808 m** |

So the *placement* arithmetic is fine — it put the site at 387 and 346 blocks, both inside
the band. What breaks the promise is the fallback in `WorldgenSettlements.tickAnchor`: on a
refusal it sweeps `grid.near(..., FALLBACK_REGIONS * region)` and takes the nearest site that
will hold a town, with **no distance cap at all**. Two seeds out of two took that path, so
the fallback is the ordinary case, not the rare one, and the walk it hands you is whatever
the neighbouring region happens to offer.

Run B also shows a second problem: the home region's site was drawn as
`civilization:orc/warhost orc_ring`, a hostile. `SettlementSites.Grid.siteIn` sets
`mustBeFriendly = starter` precisely so the spawn town cannot be a warhost or a goblin camp
("a first settlement that shoots at you on sight is not an introduction to the mod, it is a
death screen"). On this seed the ground refused it, so nothing came of it — but the draw
itself ignored the flag.

Photographs: `playtest_overhead_wilbury.png` (Wilbury from y=190, noon, spectator, pitch 90),
`playtest_overhead_thornring.png` (Thornring, same shot).

## 2. Roads — PASS, two caveats

The mod's own per-plot check is unambiguous and I could not break it:

* Wilbury, `/civ info`: `roads: 305 runs, 3353 blocks, 66 of 66 buildings joined`
* Thornring, `/civ info`: `roads: 177 runs, 1621 blocks, 32 of 33 buildings joined`
* Town map → **Built** tab, six screens scrolled end to end (`playtest_lanes_built_tab.png`):
  every legible row reads `whole, on a lane`, from Town hall down to Camp post.

From above, Thornring reads as a town: one continuous ring road with spokes off it and a lane
to each building (`playtest_overhead_thornring.png`). That is what the Millbrook reference
looks like. Wilbury reads less well, but that is terrain rather than the road code — its
buildings span 33 blocks of height (inn y=72, camp post y=105) and the ring is cut into arcs
by the hillside.

**Caveat 1 — nothing is there on arrival.** The first overhead, taken a minute after joining
before I stepped anything, shows no road at all: bare grass between scattered buildings
(`playtest_overhead_on_arrival.png`). At that moment `roads=105/977, opened=69`. A player who
walks to the world's one guaranteed town sees cabins in a field, not a village; the streets
appear over the following hundreds of steps.

**Caveat 2 — planned is not laid.** I sampled 20 planned runs from `PLAN R`/`PLAN RS` and
probed each run's midpoint column with `execute if block <x> <y> <z> minecraft:dirt_path`
across ±3 y. **10 of 20 had real `dirt_path` on the ground.** Every miss was an outer run that
the network had not opened yet — e.g. (−691, −507), (−607, −651), (−644, −293), (−540, −636).
Nothing was opened-but-unlaid, so the fault is pacing rather than a hole in `PathLayer`.

Survey files written: `surveys/playtest-wilbury.json`, `surveys/playtest-thornring.json`.

## 3. Night — FAIL

**Doors: UNVERIFIED, because there is no door.** A clone-filtered sweep of Thornring
(x −560..−352, z −650..−432, y 55..112) counted **0 blocks in `#minecraft:doors`**. Every
building in these towns is drawn rather than placed from an authored blueprint, so every
doorway is a gap. There is nothing in either town for bedtime to shut, and I saw none hung.

**Walking home: partly.** `/civ info` at `time set 13000`:
`night: 12 asleep, 14 could not reach a bed, 52 awake (13 with something inside notice, 13 called by the bell), 27 with no body`.
So people do go to bed. But at `time set 18000` there was **not one citizen within 60 blocks
of the town centre** — `@e[distance=..60]` returned sheep, cows, a creeper and a zombie and no
people. The idle report had them standing 150–250 blocks out at their work faces
(−512, 68, −643), (−684, 65, −386), (−704, 73, −507), several flagged `CANNOT REACH BED`.
The square at night is empty (`playtest_night_square.png`, `playtest_night_houses.png`).

**Chimney smoke: not seen.** Looked at the hearth (−518, 100, −405) and at a family cottage
(−561, 86, −444) at dusk (13200) and at midnight (18000), from 12–15 blocks, standing 35–54
blocks from the town centre so the range gate was satisfied
(`playtest_night_hearth.png`). No smoke in any frame; no chimney stack is visible on any vale
roof. Worth noting whatever the cause: `Ambience.smoke` gates on
`playerWithin(settlement.center(), 64)`, so in a town 380 blocks across a player standing
beside an outlying cottage is outside the gate and that cottage can never smoke.

**Dawn bell: FAIL, and provably so.** `Ambience.findBell` scans one column — `origin.above(dy)`
for `dy` 0..24 — above each hall or watchtower. I located the actual bell in both towns by
bisecting with `clone ... filtered minecraft:bell`:

| Town | Hall origin (`PLAN B`) | Bell block | Offset |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wilbury | (−418, 74, −428) | (−412, **79**, −428) | +6 x, +5 y |
| Thornring | (−490, 66, −488) | (−484, **71**, −488) | +6 x, +5 y |

Each town has exactly one bell and it is six blocks off the searched column, so `findBell`
returns null and "the morning is silent" every morning. The offset is identical in both
towns, so this is the hall blueprint's belfry position against an origin-column search, not a
one-off. The sound itself is unverifiable headlessly; the search is not.

## 4. Rain — FAIL

Wilbury could not be used at all: `execute if biome ~ ~ ~ minecraft:savanna` → **Test passed**.
Seed 8675309's one guaranteed starter town stands in a savanna, where rain never falls.
`/weather rain` there leaves a clear blue sky (`playtest_savanna_no_rain.png`). That is a real
player-facing outcome, not just a harness problem: on this seed the rain behaviour can never
be seen in the town the world gives you.

Thornring is `minecraft:birch_forest` and does get rain. I censused every embodied citizen
within 90 blocks of the centre and asked, per person, whether any non-air block stood 2–7
above them:

| | embodied | under cover |
|---|---|---|
| clear, noon | 12 | 3 |
| 75 s into `weather rain 12000`, noon | 11 | 2 |

No movement toward shelter. Farmers and lumberjacks were still standing in the open at
(−518, 89, −562), (−515, 87, −574), (−501, 87, −597) with rain falling on them
(`playtest_rain_overhead.png` from above, `playtest_rain_ground.png` at head height — the rain
streaks are unmistakable). Guards and indoor trades also did not move, so the "stay put" half
is untestable while the "go inside" half does nothing.

`/civ info` **does** name pastimes, so that part of the item passes:
`Puttoc Ringwood [builder] still 146s at -498,68,-641, hunger 25 well fed, errand none, at leisure (fence), SITE STEERING NOBODY`.
Under rain no idle person had one at all.

## 5. People — PASS

**Faces vary.** Across the close-ups: a classic Steve (`playtest_face_closeup.png`), a
dark-skinned settler in a red tunic (`playtest_nameplates_15m.png`), a purple-hooded one and a
yellow-tunic one side by side (`playtest_nameplates_7m.png`).

**Nameplates.** At ~7 blocks all three citizens in front of me carried a plate
(`playtest_nameplates_7m.png`); at ~15 blocks the citizen in frame carried none
(`playtest_nameplates_15m.png`). The rule is `PersonEntityManager.NAMEPLATE_RANGE = 8.0` and it
is **distance only** — not "under the crosshair". Every citizen inside 8 blocks shows a plate,
and when three stand together the plates overlap into unreadable mush:
`BrihtwaCuthberRingwJoaderTrader` in the 7-block shot.

**Greetings on right-click** (five settlers, `RCLICK` with an empty hand at ≤3 blocks):

* Aethelric Sheepwash — Farmer: *"It's all coming in at once. It always does."*
* Brihtwald Meadows — Miner: *"Not now. Get inside."*
* Cuthbert Ringwood — Trader: *"Something's out there. Move."*
* Nothelm Croftwell — Builder / Leofwine Windlesham — Guard / Puttoc Combe 2 — Trader: *"Get indoors!"*

They work, and they are context-aware — which is also the problem: Wilbury is under near
continuous raid alarm (`[step 1302] Raiders sighted — 16 attackers approach Wilbury`,
`[step 1303] The bell is rung`, again at 1352), so four lines in five are alarm lines and a
player would think that is all anybody says. `/civ threat 0` bought one ordinary line before
the next raid.

**Sneak-right-click** opens a proper person panel (`playtest_sneak_panel.png`): name and trade,
`Farmer · hunger 21/99 (well fed)`, the inventory (Torch 7, Rotten Flesh 4, Bread 1),
"Enough food to undo 15 hunger", the current errand, and
"Their own belongings; nothing can be taken." One line contradicts another in the same panel:
the header says *well fed* while the errand underneath says
*"too weak to work — gone to the granary to eat"*.

## 6. Signs and sounds — FAIL

I swept both towns for sign blocks with `clone ... filtered #minecraft:all_signs` in
27 000-block boxes (validated against a planted control sign):

* **Wilbury**, x −720..−330, z −700..−270, y 60..130: **1 sign**, at (−434, 75, −475), at the inn.
* **Thornring**, x −560..−352, z −650..−432, y 55..130: **1 sign**, at (−521, 85, −586), at the inn.

No square board. No crossing post — in a town with 305 road runs and six gates. No headstone,
in a town whose history reads `Aldgyth Thatcher was killed` and `Mildred Stockwell was killed`.
`FurnishingStyle.raises` says VALE raises SQUARE, SIGNPOST, INN_SIGN and GRAVE, so all four
were meant to be there.

And the one sign that exists is **blank**. `data get block`:

```
-434, 75, -475: front_text: {messages: ["", "", "", ""]}, back_text: {messages: ["", "", "", ""]}
-521, 85, -586: front_text: {messages: ["", "", "", ""]}, back_text: {messages: ["", "", "", ""]}
```

Both towns, all eight lines empty. `Signage.linesFor` returns `BLANK` when
`plaque == null || plaque.isBlank()`, so the plaque the town hands it is empty.
Photograph: `playtest_inn_sign_blank.png` (the inn front; the sign is the post at the lower
left of the door).

**Sounds: unverifiable headlessly**, as briefed — I have no audio device and the client log
does not record `playSound`. What I can report is that the trades exist and are staffed:
Wilbury `jobs: farmer x26 builder x19 guard x11 trader x6 lumberjack x10 miner x9 miller x5
carpenter x5 (64 visible as villagers)`, and the mill/carpentry/storehouse are all standing and
joined. I could not photograph a swing: all five carpenters and both millers were idle or
walking errands in every sample.

## 7. Caravan — FAIL

`/civ step` is available (the world was made with cheats and `Dev` is op), so this is a hard
negative rather than an unverified one.

The books say the caravan calls:

```
[step 1008] A caravan calls at the inn — 20 loaves traded for 5 iron
[step 1344] A caravan calls at the inn — 20 loaves traded for 5 iron
```

Nothing ever arrives. `InnPlanner.CARAVAN_PERIOD` is 48 and `Caravan.VISIT_STEPS` is 8, so the
wagon should be standing at the inn whenever `step mod 48 < 8`. I stepped to an exact multiple
of 48 three separate times with the player in creative, at noon, 15 and then 40 blocks from
the inn (−434, 72, −470), and polled every second for the whole window and past it:

* `@e[type=minecraft:llama, distance=..200]` — empty, every second, every window.
* `@e[type=minecraft:wandering_trader, distance=..120]` — empty.
* `@e[tag=civilization_caravan, distance=..400]` — empty for ten consecutive seconds spanning steps 1498–1512.

No `Caravans` line of any kind appears in the server log. The trade is settled in the
simulation and the view never puts a body on the ground.

## Faults, worst first

**F1 — The world's one guaranteed town is a 750-block walk, not a 250–500 one.**
Both seeds: the spawn region's site fell inside the 256–512 band (387 m and 346 m) and was
refused for ground, and the fallback then took the nearest scattered site with no distance cap
— 747 m in run A, 808 m in run B. The band is documented in `PLAYING.md` and asserted in
`SettlementSites.STARTER_MIN/MAX_FROM_SPAWN`; the fallback in
`WorldgenSettlements.tickAnchor` (the `FALLBACK_REGIONS` sweep, ~line 191) never consults it.
Two refusals out of two also says the refusal is the common case, so this is what most worlds
actually do. *Likely file:* `neoforge/src/main/java/com/civilization/neoforge/world/WorldgenSettlements.java`.

**F2 — No signs anywhere except one blank one at the inn.**
One sign per town, both blank on both faces; no square board, no crossing post, no headstone,
in towns with 305 and 177 road runs and two recorded deaths. This is the whole of item 6.
*Likely files:* `common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/work/Signage.java` (the plaque comes
back blank) and whatever builds the `Signage.Plaque`; plus the furnishing pass that is meant to
raise `Piece.SQUARE`, `Piece.SIGNPOST` and `Piece.GRAVE`
(`common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/work/Furnishings.java`).

**F3 — The caravan never appears.**
Trade settles in the books on schedule; no trader entity, no llamas, no
`civilization_caravan`-tagged entity, ever, across three visit windows with a player 15–40
blocks from the inn. *Likely file:*
`neoforge/src/main/java/com/civilization/neoforge/view/Caravans.java` — `arrive` refusing (the
edge is null or its chunk unread) or `TownEdge.of` returning null;
`common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/settlement/TownEdge.java` returns null unless some
run is opened **and** walkable.

**F4 — The dawn bell can never ring.**
`Ambience.findBell` searches only the column above the building origin; the hall's bell sits at
+6 x, +5 y from that origin in both towns — (−412, 79, −428) in Wilbury, (−484, 71, −488) in
Thornring. The search returns null and the morning is silent.
*Likely file:* `neoforge/src/main/java/com/civilization/neoforge/view/Ambience.java`, `findBell`.

**F5 — Rain does nothing.**
Under real rain in a birch forest, cover went from 3 of 12 to 2 of 11 embodied citizens; farmers
and lumberjacks stood in the open at (−518, 89, −562), (−515, 87, −574), (−501, 87, −597) for
75 s. No idle person took a pastime while it rained, even though pastimes demonstrably fire in
the dry (`at leisure (fence)`). *Likely file:*
`common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/person/Leisure.java` — the weather rule is in there
and nothing downstream acted on it.

**F6 — `/civ step 800` kills a dedicated server.**
Watchdog crash, `crash-2026-09-19_04.28.21-server.txt`: "a single server tick took 60.00
seconds". The stack is all layout:
`Ways.pointToSegment` ← `TownPlan$Street.touches` ← `PlannedLayout.fits` ← `finish` ← `planFor`
← `plotFor` ← `Settlement.chooseSite` ← `relocateIfUnsuitable` ← `advanceBuildQueue` ←
`Settlement.step`. 800 steps in 60 s is ~75 ms per simulation step for a 47-person town with
~200 street segments — a full layout re-plan per relocation check, on a town that settled
hundreds of steps ago. At the natural 1 step/s that is a 75 ms server hitch every second, and
`/civ step` with any large count is a loaded gun.
*Likely files:* `common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/culture/PlannedLayout.java`,
`common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/settlement/Settlement.java` (`relocateIfUnsuitable`,
~line 4611).

**F7 — The starter site can be drawn hostile.**
Run B's home region r(−1,0) held `civilization:orc/warhost orc_ring`, printed by `/civ sites`
as the spawn region's site. `SettlementSites.Grid.siteIn` sets `mustBeFriendly = starter` to
prevent exactly this. The ground refused it here, so no warhost was raised — but the draw
ignored the flag, and on a seed where the ground accepts, the wayfinder points a new player
into a warhost.
*Likely file:* `common/src/main/java/com/civilization/sim/worldgen/SettlementSites.java`,
`siteIn` → `peopleWhoBuild` / `arrangementFor`.

**F8 — No chimney smokes unless you stand in the middle of the town.**
`Ambience.smoke` gates on `playerWithin(settlement.center(), 64)`. Wilbury's buildings span 174
blocks of x and 145 of z; a player standing next to a cottage on the edge is 70–100 blocks from
the centre and that cottage's fire is simply off. I saw no smoke at all from inside the gate
either, at dusk or midnight, on a hearth and on a family home.
*Likely file:* `neoforge/src/main/java/com/civilization/neoforge/view/Ambience.java`, `smoke`.

**F9 — The town centre is deserted at night; people sleep where they work.**
At `time set 18000` there were zero citizens inside 60 blocks of (−503, 108, −457) while the
idle report had them 150–250 blocks out, several `CANNOT REACH BED at (−586, 84, −625)` etc.,
and `14 could not reach a bed`. Whatever else is right about the night, the picture a player
gets is an empty village and a scatter of people standing still in the dark at the tree line.

**F10 — Overlapping nameplates are unreadable.**
Three citizens within 8 blocks render three plates on top of each other:
`BrihtwaCuthberRingwJoaderTrader` (`playtest_nameplates_7m.png`). The range rule
(`NAMEPLATE_RANGE = 8.0`) is right; the collision handling is missing. The brief expected
"under the crosshair" as well — that is not implemented at all, it is distance only.
*Likely file:* `neoforge/src/main/java/com/civilization/neoforge/view/PersonEntityManager.java`,
`nameplate`.

**F11 — Buildings still come up buried.**
`/civ audit` on Wilbury: hearth (−518, 100, −405), lumber camp (−476, 97, −382) and mine
(−524, 70, −561) all "buried — the ground stands up to 3 above its floor on every side" (6 for
the mine). Three of 36 at that moment.

**F12 — Wilbury clear-cut itself.**
Town map at step 1006: `0 trees standing, 591 coming up`. The forest inside the claim is gone
and the town is a bowl of stepped bare-dirt terraces (`playtest_night_houses.png`). Thornring
at step 392 still had `32 trees standing, 181 coming up`, so this is what a few hundred more
steps does.

**F13 — Litter and name recycling.**
The server log runs continuous `ITEMPOP` lines — torches, saplings, sticks, wheat seeds,
leather — dropping around the town, e.g. eight `minecraft:wheat_seeds` at (−483, 68, −27x)
inside four seconds. And the name pool recycles with a counter: `Beorn Hayward 2`,
`Herewald Reeve 2`, `Puttoc Combe 2` were all walking around Wilbury at once.

**F14 — `/civ sites` advertises a culture the raise then overrides.**
Run A listed `r(-1,-1) ... civilization:goblin/mire goblin_camp` and the town raised on that
very site was `Wilbury ... civilization:human/vale laid out as ring_streets` — correctly, because
`resolve` re-draws a goblin camp that did not land in goblin country. But the "not raised yet"
row a player reads promises a goblin camp and delivers a human town.

**F15 — One person panel contradicts itself.**
`Farmer · hunger 21/99 (well fed)` over `Errand: too weak to work — gone to the granary to eat`
(`playtest_sneak_panel.png`).
